# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Academic researchers (university researchers, graduate students) conducting literature reviews. They work at desks, often on large monitors, during focused research sessions. Users are familiar with academic tools but want something more modern and efficient than traditional reference managers.

## Product Purpose

AI-assisted literature review platform that helps researchers discover, organize, and synthesize academic papers. Users describe a research topic, and the platform fans out across multiple paper sources (arXiv, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, Tavily), clusters results into thematic regions, scores research gaps between clusters, and renders the landscape as an interactive D3 visualization alongside a synthesized report.

Success looks like: researchers efficiently covering a literature landscape, identifying unexplored areas, and producing shareable synthesis reports.

## Brand Personality

Clean, Professional, Trustworthy

The interface should feel like a precision tool - refined, capable, no-nonsense. Think Linear meets academic reference management. The visual language is Apple-clean with glassmorphism, but grounded and data-focused rather than decorative.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates - no cookie-cutter landing page aesthetics
- Overly complex dense interfaces - avoid academic software clutter
- Flashy/playful - this is a serious research tool, not a consumer app

## Design Principles

1. **Data first** - The visualization and data presentation are the core value. Design supports, never distracts from, the research content.

2. **Progressive disclosure** - Show summary views first, allow drilling into detail. Don't overwhelm with everything at once.

3. **Expert confidence** - Target users are knowledgeable researchers. Respect their expertise with efficient workflows and don't over-explain.

4. **Visual clarity** - Complex multi-source data needs clean presentation. Use whitespace, hierarchy, and the glassmorphism sparingly for structural clarity.

5. **Keyboard-friendly** - Researchers are keyboard-centric. Ensure efficient keyboard navigation patterns (Linear inspiration).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (disable animations for users who need it)
- Standard color contrast ratios for text readability
- Focus states for keyboard navigation
