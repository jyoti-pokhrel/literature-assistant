002 · Process

From a query, to a landscape, to a gap.

  1. 01

    Query

    One search, three indexes.

    Semantic Scholar, arXiv, and OpenAlex are searched in parallel. Results are deduplicated and ranked. No manual cross-checking.

  2. 02

    Cluster

    The corpus shapes itself.

    Embeddings group papers into thematic regions. Each cluster gets a name, a size, and a position in the landscape.

  3. 03

    Gap

    Read the silences.

    Between dense regions lie the gaps. Each one is scored on novelty, support, and actionability, and traces back to the citations that suggest it.
